# Modifying Rolls or using Spellburn with Ctrl/Command Click

You can modify rolls on the fly by CTRL (PC) or Command (MAC) clicking a roll. This will allow you to modify many aspects of the roll. You can do this with any roll on the character sheet, and some rolls have their own options to modify.

![Ctrl click](images/ctrl_click.png)

You can even use Spellburn with spells, and they will reduce physical stats.

![Spellburn](images/spellburn.png)

## Burning Luck

Attacks, saving throws, ability checks, skill checks and spell checks opened this way also show a **Luck Burn** term for player characters. Use its + and − buttons to choose how many points of Luck to burn; the field shows the bonus that buys and the box under it shows the Luck you will have left. The Luck is spent when you click Roll.

- Most characters get +1 per point burned.
- Halflings get +2 per point (good luck charm).
- Thieves roll their Luck Die once per point burned (the die is set on the Thief tab). The dice are rolled when you click Roll, and the chat card shows the total with the dice next to it, e.g. `+5[Luck Burn 2d3]`.

With the Ability Score Log enabled, each burn is logged as a Luck spend, so thieves and halflings can recover it from the log.
